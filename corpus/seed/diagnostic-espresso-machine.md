# Espresso machine diagnostic tree

Start at the symptom that matches the complaint. Each leaf names the single
most likely cause first.

## Symptom: no water reaches the group head

1. Is the pump audible?
   - No: check the reservoir float switch, then the pump capacitor. A silent
     pump with a lit power lamp is a float switch in 70 percent of cases.
   - Yes: continue to 2.
2. Is the water path blocked?
   - Remove the shower screen. Descale the group if scale is visible.
   - If clear, the pump is cavitating: prime it by running the hot water tap
     for 20 seconds with the reservoir full.

## Symptom: shot pulls too fast, thin and pale

1. Grind is too coarse in most cases. Step the grinder finer one mark and
   re-dose.
2. If the grind is already fine, check the dose. Under-dosing a 58 mm basket
   below 17 grams produces a fast, thin shot regardless of grind.
3. If dose and grind are correct, suspect a channeled puck: check distribution
   and tamp level before touching machine settings.

## Symptom: shot pulls too slow or chokes

1. Grind is too fine in most cases. Step coarser one mark.
2. Check for a clogged basket: backflush with detergent, then inspect.
3. A machine that chokes only when hot points at a swollen group gasket.

## Symptom: steam wand produces wet, spitting steam

1. Boiler is below temperature: wait for the ready lamp and purge the wand.
2. Purge condensate for two seconds before every use. Persistent wet steam
   after purging indicates a leaking steam valve seat.
